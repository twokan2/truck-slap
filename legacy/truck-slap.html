HOW TO PLAY

Truck Slap is a fast-paced card game for 2–6 players on separate devices!

THE WORD CYCLE

Every round, a word is called out in this repeating order:

Dump Truck Fire Truck Monster Truck Tow Truck Ice Cream Truck

BASIC PLAY

Each round a card flips automatically for all players. If the card matches the current word — SLAP! Hit the big red button as fast as you can. The fastest slapper wins the round and gets points.

If the card does NOT match — don't slap! Slapping on a non-match costs you points.

SPECIAL CARDS — BAO BAO & ROXI

Sometimes a dog card appears! When you see Bao Bao or Roxi, you must tap the paw button 3 times as fast as you can THEN slap. Fastest player who completes the taps + slap wins bonus points!

SCORING

✅ Correct slap (fastest): +100 pts (minus reaction ms)
✅ Correct slap (not fastest): +25 pts
🐕 Special card (fastest): +75 pts
❌ Wrong slap: -50 pts
😴 Missed match: 0 pts

WINNING

After all cards are played, the leaderboard shows final rankings, reaction times, round-by-round results, and best streaks!
